About me

Hi, I'm Asriel!

I'm a game developer, designer and artist. I work across the whole process: designing systems, building UI, writing stories and prototyping ideas fast to find what's fun. I've released several games on itch.io, I have a Steam game in progress, and I'm leading a project with Snotdribble Studios.

I'm looking for remote roles in the games industry.
